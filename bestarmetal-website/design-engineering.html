What We Do With Your Idea

Reference image evaluation

We study your photos, sketches or samples to understand the product and its function.

CAD and 3D modeling

We build a 3D design of the product for review and confirmation.

Engineering drawings

We prepare drawings that can be used for manufacturing.

Structural optimization

We evaluate the structure and suggest changes to improve manufacturability.

Material suggestions

We suggest materials and fabrication methods that suit your product and finish.

Manufacturing feasibility

We check how the product can be produced before committing to production.

Complex prototype development

We develop samples for complex custom structures.

Manufacturing support

We continue to support you from design into production.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I send to start a project?

Reference photos, hand sketches, existing physical samples, concept drawings, or complete technical drawings, together with product dimensions and functional requirements if you have them.

Do you provide CAD and 3D design?

Yes. We support CAD product design, 3D modeling and engineering drawings for manufacturing.

Can you help improve my design for manufacturing?

Yes. We can evaluate the structure and suggest improvements for manufacturability, plus material and fabrication suggestions.

What happens after I send my idea?

We review your references and requirements, confirm what is needed, and discuss design, prototype and production options for your project.
