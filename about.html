About BESTAR METAL

BESTAR METAL is the brand of Shenzhen Bestar Tek Co. Ltd. We support international brands, importers and businesses with custom metal product development, engineering coordination, prototyping and manufacturing project management.

Our customers keep ownership of their brand and specifications. We work in English and focus on clear, written communication with overseas B2B buyers.

Our OEM/ODM Services

OEM manufacturing: you provide the design, drawings or specifications, and we manage manufacturing under your brand.

ODM development: you provide an idea, reference photo or sketch, and we help develop the product and the manufacturing approach.

  • Customer-owned branding
  • CAD/3D and engineering support
  • Prototyping
  • Manufacturing coordination and quality inspection coordination

From Concept to Production

Six stages, each confirmed with you before the next.

  1. Share Your IdeaPhoto, sketch, sample or drawing

  3. CAD & Technical Development3D models and drawings when required
  4. Prototype DevelopmentPhysical sample and finish check

  6. Manufacturing & Quality CoordinationProduction, inspection, shipping
