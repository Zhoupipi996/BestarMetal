About BESTAR METAL

An OEM/ODM partner for overseas B2B customers who want custom metal products made under their own brand.

Company Introduction

BESTAR METAL is the brand of Shenzhen Bestar Tek Co. Ltd. We help international brands, importers and businesses develop and manufacture custom metal products through engineering support, prototyping and coordinated production.

BESTAR METAL coordinates custom product development and manufacturing through its authorized production partner in Dongguan, China. Our partner is Dongguan Zhiqin Yipin Hardware Co., Ltd.. BESTAR METAL does not claim to be the designer or intellectual property owner of every product shown on this website.

How We Work

Details on each stage are on the Design & Engineering and Prototyping pages.

Engineering support

We review dimensions, structure, materials and feasibility, and prepare CAD/3D models and drawings when required.

Prototyping

A physical sample is developed for your review and approval.

Coordinated production

We coordinate production, inspection and shipping arrangements with the partner workshop.

Clear communication

We confirm requirements, drawings, samples and production details with you in writing.
