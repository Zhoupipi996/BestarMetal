About BESTAR METAL

An international custom manufacturing partner helping customers develop and manufacture customized metal products.

Company Introduction

BESTAR METAL is the brand of Shenzhen Bestar Tek Co. Ltd. We help international B2B customers turn product ideas, reference images, sketches and technical drawings into manufacturable metal products.

Our factory is in Dongguan, Guangdong, China.

How We Work

Manufacturing capabilities

Laser cutting, bending, welding, grinding and polishing, with surface finishing arranged through in-house capabilities and qualified external suppliers. See manufacturing.

Design and engineering support

CAD and 3D design, engineering drawings, structural optimization and complex prototypes. See design and engineering.

Product development approach

We start from what you have, whether images, sketches, samples or drawings, and work toward a manufacturable product.

Customer-oriented collaboration

We confirm product requirements, drawings, samples and production details with you throughout the project.
