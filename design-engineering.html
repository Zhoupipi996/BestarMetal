BESTAR METAL supports customers who do not have complete technical drawings: from first reference to CAD/3D design, engineering drawings and prototype.

Share your reference images, sketches or product samples. Our team can help evaluate the structure, develop CAD/3D designs and prepare engineering drawings for manufacturing.

What You Can Send

Any of these is enough to begin. The more you can share, the faster we can evaluate.

Reference photos

Pictures of a similar product or the style you want.

Hand sketches

Rough drawings, even without dimensions.

Existing samples

A product you want to copy or improve.

Technical drawings

Complete drawings, dimensions and functional requirements.

CAD and 3D Modeling

Engineering Drawings and Structural Optimization

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I send to start a project?

Reference photos, hand sketches, existing physical samples, concept drawings, or complete technical drawings, together with product dimensions and functional requirements if you have them.

Do you provide CAD and 3D design?

Yes. We support CAD product design, 3D modeling and engineering drawings for manufacturing.

Can you help improve my design for manufacturing?

Yes. We can evaluate the structure and suggest improvements for manufacturability, plus material and fabrication suggestions.

What happens after I send my idea?

We review your references and requirements, confirm what is needed, and discuss design, prototype and production options for your project.
