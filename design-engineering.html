From Your Idea to a Manufacturable Design.

OEM manufacturing to your drawings, or ODM development from a reference photo or sketch, with engineering support, prototyping and coordinated production.

Start With an Idea, Not Necessarily a Drawing

Six stages from your first reference to coordinated production.

  1. Share Your Idea

    Send a reference photo, sketch, physical sample or technical drawing, plus any dimensions and functional requirements you already have. You do not need finished drawings to begin. Tell us whether you want OEM manufacturing to your design or ODM development support.

    Start the conversation with what you already have.

  2. Engineering Review

    We review dimensions, structure, materials and manufacturing feasibility together with our production partner, and tell you what needs to be clarified or adjusted before work starts. Reviewing these points early helps avoid design changes later in the project.

    Fewer surprises before samples are made.

  3. CAD & Technical Development

    When required, we prepare CAD/3D models and engineering drawings, so structure, dimensions and construction details can be confirmed before a physical sample is made. If you already have drawings, we check them against manufacturing needs.

    A clear, shared technical reference for everyone.

  4. Prototype Development

    A physical sample is developed to check the metal frame, assembly and surface finish. Complex structures can be evaluated in sample form before production is committed, subject to project feasibility. The photo shows metal framework in the partner workshop.

    See and test the product before you order.

  5. Sample Review

    You review the sample and give feedback. Structural or finish adjustments are made as needed, and the approved sample becomes the reference for production. The number of review rounds and the timing are agreed for each project.

    Production follows a sample you have approved.

  6. Manufacturing & Quality Coordination

    After sample approval, we coordinate production with the partner workshop, arrange inspection against the approved sample and drawings, and support shipping arrangements. Order quantity, timing and terms are confirmed in your quotation. The photo shows fabricated metal panels.

    One point of contact from sample to shipment.

What to Send Us

  • A reference photo, sketch, physical sample or technical drawing
  • Dimensions and functional requirements, if you have them
  • Product category and whether you need OEM or ODM
  • Material and finish preferences, if any
  • Estimated order quantity and expected timeline

How We Confirm Customization

Customization options such as materials, finishes, logo and packaging depend on the product. We confirm what is feasible during engineering review and record it in your quotation. Please send drawings and reference images by email after the first contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I send to start an ODM or OEM project?

A reference photo, sketch, physical sample or technical drawing, together with any dimensions and functional requirements you already have.

Do you provide CAD and 3D design?

Yes, when required. We prepare CAD/3D models and engineering drawings so structure and dimensions can be confirmed before a sample is made.

Can you help improve a design for manufacturing?

Yes. We review dimensions, structure, materials and feasibility with our production partner and suggest adjustments before work starts.

Who owns the design?

Ownership and permission to reproduce a design must be clarified at the start of the project. Please tell us whether the design is yours or a third party's.
