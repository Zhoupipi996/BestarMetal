OEM & ODM Services

OEM: you provide the design, drawings or specifications. We coordinate engineering review, prototyping and manufacturing under your brand.

ODM: you provide an idea, reference photo or sketch. We help develop the product design and the manufacturing approach, then coordinate the sample and production.

Buyer Questions

Can you manufacture under my own brand?

Yes. BESTAR METAL supports OEM and ODM projects for overseas B2B customers who want products made under their own brand and specifications. Available customization options depend on the product and are confirmed for each project.

Can I start with a reference photo or sketch?

Yes. You can start with a reference photo, sketch, physical sample or technical drawing. We review dimensions, structure and feasibility, and prepare CAD/3D models or engineering drawings when required.

Who manufactures the products?

Production is carried out by our authorized production partner, Dongguan Zhiqin Yipin Hardware Co., Ltd., in Dongguan, China. BESTAR METAL coordinates engineering support, prototyping and production for your project.

What should I send to get a quotation?

Send your product category, a project description, a reference photo, sketch or drawing, your OEM or ODM requirement, estimated order quantity and expected timeline. Quotation requirements are determined after we review your project.
