OEM & ODM Services

OEM: you provide the design, drawings or specifications. We manage engineering review, prototyping and manufacturing under your brand.

ODM: you provide an idea, reference photo or sketch. We help develop the product design and the manufacturing approach, then manage the sample and production.

Buyer Questions

Can you manufacture under my own brand?

Yes. BESTAR METAL supports OEM and ODM projects for overseas B2B customers who want products made under their own brand and specifications. Available customization options depend on the product and are confirmed for each project.

Can I start with a reference photo or sketch?

Yes. You can start with a reference photo, sketch, physical sample or technical drawing. We review dimensions, structure and feasibility, and prepare CAD/3D models or engineering drawings when required.

How is quality checked?

Inspection is coordinated against agreed requirements: drawing and specification review, dimensional and visual finish inspection, welding and assembly checks, sample comparison and pre-shipment checks. Requirements are confirmed for each project.

What should I send to get a quotation?

Send your product type, a project description, a reference photo, sketch or drawing, your OEM or ODM requirement, estimated order quantity and expected timeline. Quotation requirements are determined after we review your project.
