Frequently Asked Questions

What does BESTAR METAL do?

BESTAR METAL, operated by Shenzhen Bestar Tek Co. Ltd., is a custom manufacturing partner for international B2B customers. We support custom metal product design, CAD/3D modeling, engineering drawings, prototyping and manufacturing.

Can I start a project without complete technical drawings?

Yes. You can start with reference photos, hand sketches, physical samples or concept drawings. Our team can help evaluate the structure, develop CAD/3D designs and prepare engineering drawings for manufacturing.

Which materials can you work with?

Stainless steel is a core material, and other metals can be considered according to project requirements. Metal products can also be combined with wood, glass and sintered stone / rock slab.

Do you accept small-batch orders?

We support customized products and small-batch production, subject to project evaluation. Feasibility, MOQ and lead time depend on the product design, materials, finishing requirements and order quantity.

Are all surface finishes done in-house?

No. We support different surface finishes through in-house capabilities and qualified external suppliers, and we coordinate the finishing route for your project.

Where is the factory located?

The factory address is Room 101, Building 5, No. 15 Chaodong Road, Chashan Town, Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, China.
