Privacy Policy

How BESTAR METAL handles information sent through this website.

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Who we are

This website is operated under the brand BESTAR METAL by Shenzhen Bestar Tek Co. Ltd. Contact: marketing@bestarmetal.com.

Information we receive

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, company, email address, country, product type, project description, estimated quantity and any files you email to us.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your business inquiry and to discuss your project.

Inquiry form

The inquiry form is designed to be processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service, once it has been activated on this website. Until then, the form does not send data to any server. Information sent through Formspree is handled under that service's own terms and privacy policy.

Email and WhatsApp

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, your message is handled by those services and by us, subject to their own terms and policies.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Your requests

To ask about, correct or delete information you have sent us, email marketing@bestarmetal.com.
