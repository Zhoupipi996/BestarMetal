OEM & ODM Services

What is the difference between OEM and ODM?

In OEM you provide the design or specifications and we manage manufacturing to them. In ODM you provide an idea, reference or sketch and we help develop the product as well as manage manufacturing.

Can you manufacture under my own brand?

Yes. Products are developed under your brand and specifications. Branding options such as logo marking depend on the product and are confirmed per project.

Materials & Surface Finishes

Which materials can you work with?

Stainless steel and other metals according to project requirements. Metal can be combined with wood, glass and sintered stone / rock slab.

Materials and finishes →

Which surface finishes are available?

Grinding and polishing, spray finishing and PVD plating can be arranged. Availability depends on design, material and quantity and is confirmed per project.

Materials and finishes →

Are all finishes done in-house?

No. We support finishes through in-house capabilities and qualified external suppliers, and we confirm the route for your project.

Can metal be combined with stone, wood or glass?

Yes, subject to feasibility. The frame has to support the top, and edge and fixing details are agreed with you.

Manufacturing & Quality Control

Which manufacturing processes do you support?

Laser cutting, metal bending and forming, welding and assembly, grinding and polishing, spray finishing, PVD plating and multi-material integration. Suitability depends on the product.

How is quality checked?

Inspection is coordinated against agreed requirements: drawing and specification review, dimensional and visual finish inspection, welding and assembly checks, sample comparison and pre-shipment checks.
