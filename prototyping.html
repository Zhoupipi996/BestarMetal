Your Project, Step by Step

The usual journey. Each step is confirmed with you before the next.

  1. Idea / DrawingYou share references, sketches, samples or drawings.
  2. FeasibilityWe check how the product can be made.
  3. EngineeringCAD/3D and drawings for manufacturing.
  4. PrototypeA sample to check structure and finish.
  5. ReviewYou review the sample; we adjust if needed.
  6. Small-Batch ManufacturingProduction after evaluation and confirmation.
