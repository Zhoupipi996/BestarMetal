Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

MOQ is not the same for every product. Feasibility, MOQ, engineering fees, prototype costs and production lead times are evaluated by project, according to product design, materials, finishing requirements and order quantity.

How long does prototyping take?

It depends on the structure, materials and finishing. We confirm timing after reviewing your drawings or references.
