What You Can Customize

Subject to feasibility and quotation. Not every product supports every option.

Custom dimensions

Sizes, heights and proportions to your drawing or reference.

Structural configurations

Frame shapes, joints and construction, reviewed for manufacturability.

Material options

Stainless steel and other metals, plus wood, glass or sintered stone for multi-material products.

Surface finishes

Finishes such as grinding, polishing, spray finishing and PVD plating, confirmed per project.

Brand customization

Logo and brand marking options, where feasible for the product.

Packaging requirements

Packaging needs discussed and confirmed in the quotation.

Configurations and manufacturing feasibility depend on project requirements. Please tell us whether a reference design is yours or a third party's; the right to reproduce a design must be clarified at the start. See the buyer FAQ or how to prepare a custom metal RFQ.
