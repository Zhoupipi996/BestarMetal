How to Prepare a Custom Metal RFQ That Gets an Accurate Quote

The information that helps a manufacturer review your project, and the mistakes that slow it down.

By BESTAR METAL · Published 9 November 2026 · Updated 9 November 2026

Short answer

Send the product type and purpose, your drawings, sketches or reference photos, dimensions, material and finish preferences, estimated quantity and timeline, and any branding and packaging requirements. Missing information usually leads to questions before a quotation, not to a faster quote.

RFQ checklist

ItemWhy it mattersWhat to write
Product type and useDefines structure and finish needsExample: round coffee table for hospitality use, indoors
Drawings, sketch or photosShows what to makeAttach by email; mention if the design is yours or a reference
DimensionsAffects materials and constructionOverall size and any critical dimensions
MaterialsAffects cost and processStainless steel, other metals, plus wood, glass or stone if needed
Surface finishAffects process and handlingDesired look; polished, sprayed or PVD plated
OEM or ODMDefines who develops the designYour own design, or development support needed
Estimated quantityAffects feasibility and approachTrial order and expected repeat volume, if known
TimelineAffects planningTarget date and what drives it
Branding and packagingAffects design and costLogo marking, labels, carton requirements
Compliance needsMay affect materials and testingAny market-specific requirements, raised early

What affects the quotation

Design complexity, materials, finishes, multi-material integration, quantity, packaging and timeline all influence a quotation. We do not publish prices, MOQ or lead times because they are evaluated for each project.

Files to send

Send what you have: drawings, CAD files, photos or sketches. If you are unsure whether we can use a file format, tell us the format and we will confirm. The inquiry form does not accept uploads, so please email files to marketing@bestarmetal.com after submitting the form.

Common mistakes

  • Sending only a photo with no size or intended use.
  • Not saying whether a reference design belongs to you or to someone else.
  • Leaving out the finish, which can change the process.
  • Asking for a price before quantity and finish are known.
  • Forgetting packaging or labeling requirements until late in the project.

What happens next

We review your project, ask for anything missing, confirm feasibility and then prepare a quotation. For new designs, a prototype normally comes before production. Timing for each step is confirmed per project.

A short template

Product: [type and use]. Design: [own drawing / reference photo / sketch]. Size: [W x D x H]. Material and finish: [your preference]. OEM or ODM: [which]. Quantity: [estimate]. Timeline: [target]. Branding and packaging: [requirements]. Notes: [anything else].
